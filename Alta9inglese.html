Do you want to discover the city? From our reception, we can book all the experiences you wish to do. The hotel is located at Via Principe Amedeo 9, about 0.3 km from Termini Station (a 5-minute walk). Here are the main experiences available with MP Tour, with directions and walking distance from the hotel:

  • Colosseum, Roman Forum and Palatine Hill 1.6 km · 20 min walk Reach Via Cavour and follow it to Via dei Fori Imperiali: from there the Roman Forum and the Colosseum are clearly visible. Guided tours, with audio guide or with fast track entrance.
  • Trevi Fountain 1.4 km · 17 min walk Go down Via Nazionale towards Piazza Venezia and follow the signs to the fountain. Available options include walking tours and underground tours.
  • Pantheon 2 km · 25 min walk Pass by the Trevi Fountain and continue for another 0.6 km towards Via del Corso and the Pantheon.
  • Piazza Navona Underground 2.3 km · 28 min walk From the Pantheon continue for about 0.3 km to Piazza Navona.
  • Castel Sant'Angelo 3.2 km · 40 min walk From Piazza Navona continue towards the Tiber River and Ponte Sant'Angelo. Alternatively, you can use public transport from Termini.
  • Vatican (Vatican Museums, St. Peter's Basilica and Dome, Gardens) 4.5 km · 55 min walk We recommend Metro Line A from Termini (about 0.3 km from the hotel) towards Battistini, getting off at Ottaviano: from there it's about a 10-minute walk.
  • Capitoline Museums 2 km · 25 min walk Go down Via Nazionale to Piazza Venezia and walk up the Cordonata towards Piazza del Campidoglio.
  • Borghese Gallery 2.5 km · 30 min walk Head towards Via Veneto and walk up to Porta Pinciana, then enter Villa Borghese: the Gallery is located in the park.
  • Hop on Hop off, golf cart, walking tours and Food & Wine 0.3 km · 4 min walk The reception will indicate the meeting point for all the experiences you have selected.

The distances are approximate and calculated starting from the hotel. For times, duration, and availability, please contact the reception.
