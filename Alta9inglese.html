Would you like to discover the city? From our reception we can book for you all the experiences you wish to enjoy. The hotel is located at Via Principe Amedeo 9, about 0.3 km from Termini station (5 minutes on foot). Here are the main experiences available with MP Tour, with directions and walking distance from the hotel:

  • Colosseum, Roman Forum and Palatine Hill 1.6 km · 20 min walk Head to Via Cavour and follow it to Via dei Fori Imperiali: from there the Roman Forum and the Colosseum are clearly visible. Guided tours, with audio guide or fast-track entry.
  • Trevi Fountain 1.4 km · 17 min walk Walk down Via Nazionale towards Piazza Venezia and follow the signs to the fountain. A walking tour and an underground tour are available.
  • Pantheon 2 km · 25 min walk Pass by the Trevi Fountain and continue for about another 0.6 km towards Via del Corso and the Pantheon.
  • Piazza Navona Underground 2.3 km · 28 min walk From the Pantheon continue for about 0.3 km to Piazza Navona.
  • Castel Sant'Angelo 3.2 km · 40 min walk From Piazza Navona continue towards the Tiber and Ponte Sant'Angelo. Alternatively, you can use public transport from Termini.
  • Vatican (Vatican Museums, St. Peter's Basilica and Dome, Gardens) 4.5 km · 55 min walk We recommend Metro Line A from Termini (about 0.3 km from the hotel) towards Battistini, stop Ottaviano: from there it is about a 10-minute walk.
  • Capitoline Museums 2 km · 25 min walk Walk down Via Nazionale to Piazza Venezia and climb the Cordonata towards Piazza del Campidoglio.
  • Galleria Borghese 2.5 km · 30 min walk Head towards Via Veneto and walk up to Porta Pinciana, then enter Villa Borghese: the Gallery is inside the park.
  • Hop on Hop off, golf cart, walking tours and Food & Wine 0.3 km · 4 min walk Reception will tell you the meeting point for all the experiences you choose.

Distances are approximate and calculated from the hotel. For times, duration and availability, please ask at reception.
